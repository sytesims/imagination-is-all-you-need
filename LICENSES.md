## Pricing notes

- 1 credit = US$0.01, no markup; local open-weights runs cost 0. The quote is an estimate of what the provider bills. By default you pay providers directly on your own accounts (Comfy Cloud, fal, Replicate, BFL…), each under its own terms, including whether failed runs are charged; see the Spend page. In team mode the app keeps a credit balance and a failed run's credits go back to it.
- **Whole-credit rounding (FLUX 3 Image Klein):** credits are whole numbers, so per-image prices are rounded to the nearest credit (at least 1). Official prices (docs.bfl.ml/flux_3/flux3_image_klein, fal.ai Klein pages, checked 2026-10-17): 1K $0.009 → 1 credit, **2K $0.014 → 1 credit**, 4K $0.036 → 4 credits. The 2K rounding is deliberate (we under-charge by $0.004 per image) and is kept.
