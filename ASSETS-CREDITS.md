## Icons and brand marks

- `web/src/ui/Icons.tsx`: the Imagination Is All You Need icon set, drawn for this project on a 24px grid (1.5px stroke, round caps). Common glyphs (search, trash, download, upload, heart, more) follow the same conventions as [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC/MIT-style license, © Lucide Contributors) and are adapted in that spirit. No Higgsfield icons were traced or imitated. The Share sheet's destination glyphs (`XLogoIcon`, `InstagramIcon`, `TikTokIcon`) are our own simplified one-colour drawings, used only to say which site a button opens; the X, Instagram and TikTok marks remain their owners' trademarks. Ad Pack's Platform picker reuses those three and adds `YouTubeIcon` and `PinterestIcon`, whose path data comes from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.35.0 (https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.35.0/icons/youtube.svg and …/pinterest.svg; SVG data CC0 1.0), and `LinkedInIcon`, our own simple "in" drawing (LinkedIn isn't in Simple Icons). The YouTube, Pinterest and LinkedIn marks remain their owners' trademarks, shown one-colour and small only to say where an ad runs.
- `brand/logo-mark.svg`, `brand/favicon.svg` / `web/public/favicon.svg`, `brand/app-icon.svg`, `web/public/app-icon-512.png`, `web/public/apple-touch-icon.png`: the **Orbit** logo, our own design: three arcs around a core, on a 48-unit grid (small variant `brand/logo-mark-small.svg` on a 16-unit grid). Also `brand/logo-mark*.svg`, `brand/wordmark*.svg`, `brand/apple-touch-icon.*`, `brand/favicon-32.png`, `brand/social/` and `brand/video/`. Construction and rules: `docs/brand/BRAND-GUIDE.md`.
- **Space Grotesk** (SIL Open Font License 1.1, © The Space Grotesk Project Authors): the brand graphics in `brand/` set the earlier wordmark "Imagination Is All You Need" in Space Grotesk **converted to outlines**, so they embed vector shapes, not the font. The app serves it itself (see below) for display headings and for the header wordmark "Imagination" (`web/src/ui/Wordmark.tsx`), which is live text. Source: https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk · https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk. The brand graphics also use Inter and JetBrains Mono as outlines (both SIL OFL 1.1).
- **App fonts** in `web/public/fonts/`: Inter (the interface), Space Grotesk (headings and the wordmark) and Manrope (the older UI token, still named in places), the same variable woff2 files and subsets Google Fonts serves, shipped with the app so pages paint without a request to Google and work offline (`fonts.css` declares them). Each is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the licence texts sit beside the files (`OFL-manrope.txt`, `OFL-space-grotesk.txt`, `OFL-inter.txt`). Sources: https://github.com/googlefonts/manrope · https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk · https://github.com/rsms/inter.

- Generate Video camera preset posters (`server/samples/presets/video-h3-*.jpg`): procedural art drawn by `scripts/gen-example-media.py --video-presets` (PIL only). No third-party or Higgsfield assets.
- Generate Video Look › Style covers (`web/public/examples/covers/video-looks/*.webp`): our own stills, made locally with FLUX.2 [klein] 4B (Apache-2.0) from one fixed prompt and seed plus each look's phrase. No people or brands are real.
