# Release kit: timing and visuals

## Seconds from tempo
One bar of 4/4 = 4 × 60 / BPM seconds.
| BPM | 1 bar | 2 bars | 4 bars |
|---|---|---|---|
| 80 | 3.0 s | 6.0 s | 12.0 s |
| 100 | 2.4 s | 4.8 s | 9.6 s |
| 120 | 2.0 s | 4.0 s | 8.0 s |
| 140 | 1.7 s | 3.4 s | 6.9 s |
LTX clips are 2–5 s: pick the bar count that fits (e.g. 2 bars at 100 BPM ≈ 5 s). No beat detection yet: ask for the BPM, or count it from the track.

## A 30 s song in sections
| Section | Seconds | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 0–5 | the loop or cover motion |
| Verse | 5–15 | 2 clips, story images |
| Chorus | 15–25 | 2 clips, brightest look, performance shot |
| Outro | 25–30 | morph back to the cover |

## Visual prompts (own words; add the artist look and colour palette to each)
- Loop: "{cover scene}, only {smoke drifting / neon flicker / water rippling} moves, locked camera."
- Story: "{artist look} walking through {place} at {time}, slow tracking shot, {palette} light."
- Abstract: "liquid {colour} shapes pulsing slowly in the dark, macro, glossy."
- Performance: a clear front-facing still, mouth visible, plain background.

## Cutdowns
- 15 s: hook section only, first frame = the strongest image.
- 30 s: intro + chorus.
- 9:16 for Reels, Shorts, TikTok and the canvas.
