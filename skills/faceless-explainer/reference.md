## Voices (voice tool, local Chatterbox)
narrator-warm (default, friendly), narrator-crisp (news, tech), deep-calm (history, sleep), energetic-host (lists, facts), young-bright (kids, lifestyle), whisper-soft (ASMR, mystery). `describe {id:"voice/voiceover"}` gives the live list.

## Script frame (60 s ≈ 150 words)
| Part | Words | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 15 | a surprising fact or question |
| Context | 30 | why it matters |
| 3 points | 90 | one idea each, a concrete example each |
| Close | 15 | the takeaway or a question for the comments |
