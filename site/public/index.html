Made to be yours.

  • Open source, yours to keep

    Read every line, change what you like. Your pictures and films stay in a folder on your computer.

  • Your computer or the cloud

    Run open models free on your own graphics card, or reach for the cloud when you want more. Your choice, every time.

  • Every model, every price

    The real model is named on every result, and you see what a run costs before it starts.

  • Bring your AI agent

    Connect the assistant you already use. It drafts the plan; nothing paid runs until you press Go.
