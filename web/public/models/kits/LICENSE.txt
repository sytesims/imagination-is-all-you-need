props.glb and bottle.glb: pieces from CC0 kits, scaled to metres, deduped, textures resized to 1024 px (WebP), quantized and meshopt-compressed
by scripts/assets/build-scene-kits.mjs (the diorama cabins are assembled from the Holiday Kit's walls and roofs; the bottle's label is made blank).
