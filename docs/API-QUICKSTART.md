## 7. Credits back on fail  — not verified

A failed or cancelled-while-queued job gets its credits back in your balance automatically (anything the provider already billed is under its terms); the job carries `refund {refunded, amount}` and the ledger gets a `refund` entry. On the mock backend, force a failure with the form field `simulate=temporary` (also `provider_outage`, `ip`, `nsfw`; mock only):

```bash
curl -s -F types=edit -F provider=api-nano-banana -F simulate=temporary -F "prompt=refund test" -F image=@in.png $API/api/tools/create-image/jobs
curl -s $API/api/jobs/$ID      # status failed, refund {refunded:true, amount:<cost>}
curl -s $API/api/ledger        # entries[] has {kind:"refund", jobId:$ID, …}
curl -s -X POST $API/api/jobs/$ID/retry   # new job, same inputs (simulate cleared), charged again
```

**Cancel:** `POST /api/jobs/{id}/cancel`. Queued → instant cancel, credits back to your balance in team mode; with your own accounts nothing was billed (not verified). Processing single-group job → 409 `cannot_cancel_processing` (not verified). Cancel. Local (local ComfyUI) jobs can be canceled at every stage: waiting in this app's queue; submitted and pending in ComfyUI's own queue (removed from it, POST /queue delete); running (POST /interrupt, only when ComfyUI says the running prompt is this job's; nobody else's prompt is ever touched). 200 = the job, canceled (comfyCancel: removed | interrupted | not_in_queue | unreachable when a ComfyUI prompt was involved); nothing is billed. Finished first (or its last ComfyUI prompt already finished) → 409 {code:already_done}. `cancellable` on a job / queue activity item is true exactly when this call will succeed. Hosted single-group job whose request is at fal, Replicate, Comfy Router or Comfy Cloud → 200, the job canceled and the provider asked to cancel it; cancelNote says what that means for the bill (the provider's own rule: it may still bill the time it ran). Hosted single-group job on a lab API (no cancel call) already sent to its provider → 409 {code:cannot_cancel_processing, errorType:not_cancellable, cancelInProcessing:false, charged (boolean: cost > 0)} (error text mentions credits going back to your balance only when charged; a free gen says the free gen comes back; free / local jobs get plain wording) unless OPENHIGGSFIELD_CANCEL_IN_PROCESSING=1 (then real cancel, outputs discarded, credits back to your balance (team mode; with your own accounts the provider failure rule applies); anything the provider already billed is under its terms). Queued: instant cancel, credits back to your balance (team mode; with your own accounts the provider failure rule applies). Running chunked (multi-group) job: groups not yet started are skipped and their credits go back to your balance (team mode; with your own accounts the provider failure rule applies); finished groups are kept (canceledGroups)
