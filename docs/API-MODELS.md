## Pricing rules

- Rate: 1 credit = $0.01 (100 credits per USD), markup 0. Comfy partner-node prices: 211 Comfy credits = $1 ([source](https://support.comfy.org/articles/1982697177-partner-nodes-pricing)).
- Open weights: 0 credits. Open models on your computer are always free.
- Minimum price: 5 credits · draft factor 0.3 · free generations: 3 at start, 3 left, up to 720p.
- Hosted models are charged exactly what the provider publishes (per second, per minute, per image, per run or per 1K characters), shown in dollars with no markup. Comfy partner-node prices are published in Comfy credits (211 = $1) and converted to dollars; hover a price to see the credits. Rows marked estimated have no exact published figure for how we call them; the note says why. Models without a verified price show no price and can't run on that route until one is published. You pay providers directly on your own accounts, under their own terms (some don't charge for failed runs); in team mode what a failed run held goes back to the team balance.
- Price table checked: 2026-10-16.
