01

Logo: Orbit

Three arcs (video, image, audio) orbit one core: the engine that runs them all. The gaps keep the ring open, so it never reads as a closed box or a play button.

Sizes, tested on dark and light

Construction

  • 48-unit grid, ring radius 15.5
  • Arc stroke 5.5, round caps
  • 3 arcs × 80°, centred at −15°, 105°, 225°
  • 40° gaps (≈ 4 units clear between caps)
  • Core radius 6
  • Small variant (16 grid): stroke 2.3, 48° gaps, core 2.3

Clear space and minimums

  • Clear space: one core diameter (⅓ of the ring) on every side
  • Mark: 16 px minimum; use the small variant at 24 px and below
  • Lockup: 96 px wide minimum
  • Mark-to-text gap: about one core width

Don'ts

  • No stretching, skewing or rotating
  • No recolouring, gradients or second hue on the arcs
  • No shadows, glows, outlines or 3D
  • No busy or mid-tone backgrounds
  • Don't retype the wordmark in a live font

From styles.css, tokens.css and ui-states.css. Depth comes from surface steps first and shadows second.

  • Fills: white 5% rest, 9% hover, 12% pressed; press scale 0.98

05

Icon style

Matches web/src/ui/Icons.tsx: 24 × 24 viewBox, 1.5 stroke, round caps and joins, no fill, currentColor, rendered at 18 px by default. The logo is not an icon.

Object Swap · Camera Moves · Photo FX · Upscale · Elements · Re-shoot (active state in orange). Draw new icons on the 24 grid; keep 2 px corner radii; one metaphor per icon.

06

Motion

150 ms for hover and press, 200 ms for menus and toasts, 300 ms for panels. Easing cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1); cubic-bezier(0,0,.2,1) for entrances. With reduced motion: fades only.

Logo intro (≈ 1.4 s)

  • 0–600 ms: the arcs draw in from their leading caps (video, image, audio, 90 ms apart) while rotating 40° into place. Ease-out.
  • 450–800 ms: the core pops in with a small overshoot, orange first, then lights up to near-white: the engine starting.
  • 800–1100 ms: the wordmark fades in, sliding 8 px.
  • End card: arcs drift one slow turn (8 s) behind the URL; no second intro.
  • Timed so the sting's chord lands on the core.
07

Sonic branding

Described only. To be generated later with our own audio tools, keeping the prompts and seeds next to the video.

Logo sting · 2–3 s

  • Three short tones, one per arc, 90 ms apart: a soft mallet, a glassy pluck, a low warm pulse
  • They resolve on one bright, open chord as the core lights up, then a short airy tail
  • Around D major / B minor add9; no hook, no drums
  • Peak −1 dBTP, about −14 LUFS for social
  • Made with Stable Audio in the app; check the model's output licence before publishing

Demo-video music

  • 100–110 BPM, minimal modern electronic with an organic layer (felt piano or plucks, soft sub, light percussion)
  • No vocals; ducked 8–10 dB under the voice-over
  • One lift at "every tool is a workflow", ends on the sting
  • Made with ACE-Step in the app; prompt and seed saved in the repo

09

Words we use

Keep model identity, run location and billing account distinct. Avoid ambiguous credits, balance or subscription wording; identify the service that bills the user.

ToolOne thing you can do (Object Swap, Camera Moves, Upscale…). Every open tool is a ComfyUI workflow.
AppA one-click preset built on a tool (Product Shot, Transitions…).
ModelWhat you get, listed once with a clean name ("MiniMax H3"). Never includes a route, an account or "API".
Run onWhere it runs and who bills it: Auto, Local, Comfy Cloud or a direct provider.
AutoWe pick where it runs from what you've set up ("Auto · Comfy Cloud"). Never charges more than the price shown.
LocalYour own graphics card through ComfyUI. Free. Replaces "Local", "Local ComfyUI", "Local GPU", "This computer".
Comfy CloudComfy's servers with one Comfy key, billed to your Comfy account. Always one choice.
Direct providersfal, Replicate, Black Forest Labs, MiniMax, Alibaba Cloud, Luma. Optional keys; each bills you directly.
ConnectionsThe accounts and GPU you've set up (Settings → Connections).
✦ priceEstimated cost, 1 ✦ = $0.01, shown before every run. "Free" locally.
Estimate / ActualWhat we expected / what the provider reported, where its API tells us.
SpendThe log of runs: where each ran, who billed it, estimate, actual, failure rule. Not a balance.
Estimate“About $X (estimate)”: the price shown before Generate when a model has no published price; the real cost is recorded after the run.
SamplePlaceholder output when no key is set: "Sample output, nothing billed." Never passed off as a real render.
Open badgeThe model's weights are open. A model fact, shown in the model menu only.
GenerateThe one orange button. It always shows the price.
AssetsEverything you've made: results, uploads, history.
ProjectsFolders that group assets.
ElementsSaved references (a character, product or place) you reuse across tools and prompts.
RecreateOpen a result's prompt, references, model and Run on, ready to run again or tweak.
Send toPass a result to another tool as its input or as a reference.
WorkflowThe ComfyUI graph behind a tool, in workflows/: "Get workflow", "Open in ComfyUI".
10

Imagery direction

The example gallery is the first thing judges see. Curated, varied and honest about being AI-generated.

Light and colour

  • Motivated, cinematic light: golden hour, window light, night neon, soft product light
  • Natural colour with a warm bias that sits next to our orange
  • No flat midday light, no plastic "AI sheen", no purple-gradient defaults

Subjects and variety

  • Products, places, food, animals, textures, invented characters and creatures
  • Wide, medium and close-up; 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1
  • Before/after pairs, one subject across camera moves, one character across clips

Rules

  • SFW only
  • No real people's likenesses: invented faces, or ours with consent
  • No unlicensed IP: no brands, logos, film or game characters, named artists' styles
  • Label AI-generated; keep prompt, seed, model and Run on so anyone can Recreate
